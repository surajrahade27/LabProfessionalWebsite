## 2. About & History

### About the Laboratory

The Pharmaceutical Nanotechnology Research Laboratory (PNRL) at NIPER Hyderabad, directed by Dr. Neelesh Kumar Mehra, operates at the interface of advanced material science, nanotechnology, and biopharmaceutics. The laboratory focuses on engineering translational, smart nanomedicines to overcome biological barriers and solve critical solubility, permeability, and toxicity limitations of challenging therapeutics. Research thrusts encompass functionalized carbon nanotubes, surface-tailored dendrimers, polymeric/lipid hybrid nanoparticles, transferosomes, and crystalline solid dispersions. By integrating molecular biopharmaceutics, mechanistic pharmacokinetic/PBPK modeling, and targeted delivery strategies, the group develops innovative therapeutic platforms for oncology, resistant infections, ocular disorders, and chronic inflammatory diseases. PNRL bridges academic discoveries with bench-to-bedside translational drug delivery.

### Laboratory History

Established within the Department of Pharmaceutics at NIPER Hyderabad under Dr. Neelesh Kumar Mehra, the laboratory emerged as a specialized center for targeted formulation science and molecular drug delivery. Over the years, the lab has trained numerous M.S. (Pharm.) and Ph.D. scholars, publishing extensively in premier international drug delivery journals and collaborating with national laboratories, biomedical institutes, and pharmaceutical R&D facilities.

## 3. Vision, Mission & Objectives

### Vision

To become an internationally recognized center of excellence in pharmaceutical nanotechnology and targeted drug delivery, creating breakthrough nanomedicines that address unmet clinical needs.

### Mission

- Engineer biocompatible, smart nanoplatforms for targeted therapeutic delivery with minimal systemic toxicity.
- Foster rigorous interdisciplinary training for postgraduate and doctoral scholars in advanced drug delivery science, regulatory expectations, and pre-clinical formulation development.
- Bridge bench research and translational clinical technologies through industry-academia linkages and inter-institutional collaborations.

### Objectives

- Design and surface-engineer polymeric, lipid, and hybrid nano-architectures for targeted chemotherapy and immunotherapy.
- Solve poor aqueous solubility and bioavailability of BCS Class II/IV compounds via co-amorphous systems, nanocrystals, and solid lipid dispersions.
- Investigate cellular uptake, pharmacokinetic behavior, tissue distribution, and PBPK modeling of novel formulation systems.
- Develop non-invasive delivery modalities (topical, mucosal, ocular, and oral) for complex synthetic and biological molecules.

## 5. Facilities & Activities

### Laboratory Sections

- Nanoparticle Synthesis & Nano-Engineering Core
- Solid-State Characterization & Solubility Enhancement Section
- Cell Culture & In Vitro Bio-Assay Facility
- High-Performance Analytical & Chromatography Laboratory
- In Vivo Pharmacokinetics & Preclinical Evaluation Wing

### Equipment

| Equipment | Use |
| --- | --- |
| High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) | Quantitative assay, stability analysis, and pharmacokinetic drug estimation |
| Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS) & Zeta Potential Analyzer | Measurement of sub-micron hydrodynamic particle size, polydispersity index (PDI), and surface electrical charge |
| Rotary Evaporator & Lyophilizer (Freeze Dryer) | Solvent evaporation, dry powder formulation development, and moisture-controlled cake formation |
| Probe & Bath Sonicators | High-energy cavitation equipment for particle size reduction and homogeneous emulsion shearing |
| Dissolution & Franz Diffusion Test Systems | In vitro drug release kinetics and transdermal/mucosal permeation profiling |
| UV-Visible Spectrophotometer | Preliminary active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) quantification and solubilization profiling |

### Practical Activities

- Fabrication of nano-carriers: Liposomes, PLGA polymeric nanoparticles, transferosomes, dendrimer conjugates, and lipid-polymer hybrids.
- Formulation of solid dispersions, amorphous co-crystals, and nano-suspensions.
- Surface-ligand conjugation and bioconjugate chemistry for site-specific targeting.
- In vitro cellular evaluation: cytotoxicity assays, cellular uptake kinetics, flow cytometric analysis, and intracellular ROS profiling.
- Bioanalytical method validation (HPLC/LC-MS) and pharmacokinetic (PK) blood/tissue sampling analysis.
