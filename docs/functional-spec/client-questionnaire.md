# Lab Professional Website – Client Questionnaire

**Purpose:** We need these answers to write the functional specification for your website. Please answer as much as you can. "Not sure" or "Not needed" are fine answers too.

**Client name:** ____________________  **Date:** ____________________

---

## 1. About Your Lab

1. What is the full name of your lab? Do you use a short name or brand name?
2. What type of lab is it? (Pathology / Diagnostic / Clinical / Research / Imaging / Other)
3. When was it established? How many years of experience does the team have?
4. What is your mission or tagline, in one or two lines?
5. How many branches or collection centres do you have? Please list their addresses.
6. What makes your lab different from others? (Fast reports, home collection, pricing, accuracy, technology…)
7. Do you have key numbers you want to show? (Tests done, patients served, doctors you work with, etc.)

## 2. Goals of the Website

1. What is the main goal of the website? (Build trust / Get bookings / Share information / Showcase the team)
2. Who visits the site? (Patients / Doctors / Hospitals / Corporate clients / Students)
3. What is the **one action** you want visitors to take? (Call / WhatsApp / Book test / Fill form)
4. Are there any lab websites you like? Please share the links and what you like about them.
5. Are there any websites you **don't** like? What should we avoid?

## 3. Branding & Design

1. Do you have a logo? Please share it in high quality (PNG/SVG).
2. Do you have brand colours? If not, which colours do you prefer?
3. Do you have preferred fonts or style? (Modern / Clinical-clean / Warm & friendly / Premium)
4. Should the site support dark mode?
5. Which languages should the site be in? (English / Hindi / Marathi / Other)

## 4. Team / Professionals

1. Which team members should appear on the website?
2. For each person, please share:
   - Full name and title (Dr. / MD / MSc / DMLT…)
   - Qualification and specialization
   - Years of experience
   - A professional photo
   - A short bio (2–3 lines)
   - Registration or licence number (if you want it shown)
3. Should we also show support staff (technicians, phlebotomists)?

## 5. Services & Tests

1. What test categories do you offer? (Hematology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Pathology, Hormones, etc.)
2. Can you share your full test list? An Excel or PDF file is fine.
3. For each test, what should be shown?
   - [ ] Test name
   - [ ] Price
   - [ ] Sample type (Blood / Urine / etc.)
   - [ ] Report time (turnaround)
   - [ ] Preparation needed (fasting, etc.)
   - [ ] Short description
4. Should prices be shown publicly or only "on request"?
5. How often do test prices or the test list change? Who will update them?

## 6. Health Packages

1. Do you offer health packages? (Full Body Checkup, Diabetes, Senior Citizen, etc.)
2. For each package: name, included tests, price, discount price (if any)?
3. Do you offer any seasonal offers or discounts that should appear on the site?

## 7. Booking & Home Sample Collection

1. Should visitors be able to book a test or appointment online?
2. Do you offer home sample collection? Which areas or PIN codes do you cover?
3. What information do you need from the patient when they book? (Name, phone, age, test, date, time slot, address…)
4. Where should booking requests go? (Email / WhatsApp / Phone call back)
5. Do you need online payment? *(Note: this needs a payment gateway and may cost extra.)*

## 8. Reports

1. Do patients currently receive reports online? How? (Email / WhatsApp / Existing software portal)
2. Do you want a "Download Report" link on the website? If yes, which lab software do you use? *(A secure patient login is a bigger feature. We would plan it separately.)*

## 9. Contact Information

1. Phone number(s) to show
2. WhatsApp number
3. Email address
4. Full address with Google Maps location link
5. Working hours for each day, including Sundays and holidays
6. Emergency or 24×7 service? (Yes / No)
7. Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Google Business profile)

## 10. Trust & Credibility

1. Which accreditations or certifications do you have? (NABL, ISO, CAP, NABH…) Please share copies or logos.
2. Any awards or recognition?
3. Which hospitals, doctors or companies do you partner with? May we show their names or logos?
4. Can you share patient testimonials or Google reviews we may use?
5. Can you share photos of the lab, equipment and team? Or do we need a photoshoot?

## 12. Legal & Privacy

1. Do you need a Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions page?
2. Is there any disclaimer you must show? (e.g. "Reports must be interpreted by a doctor")
3. Any rules from your accreditation body about what can or cannot be shown online?

## 13. Domain, Hosting & Maintenance

1. Is the free address `labprofessionals.netlify.app` fine, or do you want your own domain (e.g. `yourlab.com`)? *(A custom domain costs about ₹800–1,500 per year.)*
2. Do you already own a domain or have an existing website?
3. After launch, who will update the content (prices, team, offers)?
4. Do you want a simple way to edit content yourself, without coding?
5. Do you want to track visitors (Google Analytics)?

## 14. Timeline

1. When do you want the website to go live?
2. Is there an event or date the launch is tied to?
3. Who is the main contact person for approvals and content?
